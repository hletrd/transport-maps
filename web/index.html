    Sources and method

    Reckoned from a planned departure: the first service is not waited for, but onward connections are.

    Door to door, not gate to gate. The reckoning includes the passage to the airport, check-in and security, border control where one is crossed, and the journey from the arrival field into the city. A flight of two hours commonly makes a passage of five.
